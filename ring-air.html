Ring AIR

Applicable Regions: Global

Overview

The Ultrahuman Ring AIR is 11x lighter and significantly sleeker than most smartwatches out on the market today, with the smallest Ultrahuman Ring AIR being as light as 2.4 grams.

It is specifically crafted to withstand demanding usage conditions. Constructed with a Titanium body and coated in Tungsten carbide, renowned for its exceptional hardness, this ring offers remarkable durability.

The Ultrahuman Ring AIR is water resistant up to 100m. You can wear it while swimming, showering, and even scuba diving. We do not recommend keeping the ring submerged underwater for more than 30 minutes. Please note this water resistance is higher than the max Ingress protection (IPX9) rating that any consumer electronic product could be rated.

Colours

The Ring AIR is available in 6 colours: Matte Grey, Aster Black, Space Silver, Bionic Gold, Raw Titanium and Brushed Rose Gold.

More Information

The Ring Air empowers users with deep insights derived from metrics such as movement data, HRV, Heart Rate and Sleep Data. These metrics, when combined with our existing ecosystem of products such as Ultrahuman M1 can provide proactive correlations to take your metabolic health to the next level.

The Ring is designed to help you understand the factors that affect your metabolism. With the ring you can:

  1. Understand your activity patterns throughout the day with the Movement Index based on metabolic equivalents (METs). It counts calories, steps etc.
  2. Decode your sleep health with the Sleep Index and understand how your sleep quality affects your metabolism.
  3. Comprehend body metrics like stress using the Body Index based on your heart rate, HRV and body temperature.

Product Overview

The Ring is our next big step towards decoding metabolic health and bringing personalized data-driven insights into everyone's reach.

The Thought & Design behind the ring

Following the Ultrahuman M1, we sought to create a device that could track a more detailed set of metabolic biomarkers while prioritizing convenience and subtlety.

The result is the Ultrahuman Ring, a wearable designed to sit comfortably and discreetly on your finger.

  • Effortless Tracking: The Ring continuously monitors your biosignals in the background, making it a truly "wear-and-forget" experience.
  • Intentionally Simple: We deliberately avoided constant notifications, screens, and complex interfaces. The Ring provides a clean, non-intrusive experience, allowing you to access your health data on your own terms through the accompanying app.

What does the ring do?

The Ultrahuman Ring is specifically designed to help you gain a deep understanding of your metabolic health. It achieves this by continuously tracking and analyzing key factors that influence your metabolism:

  • Sleep Quality
  • Movement (Activity)
  • Body Metrics: Heart Rate Variability (HRV), Heart Rate, and Body Temperature

Biomarkers for you

To make it easy for you to decode your metabolic health, we give you three different scores for each aspect of your health. These scores help you understand how to optimize your sleep, activity and recovery in a truly personal way.

The Movement Index

The Movement Index helps you understand your daily activity patterns using a metric called METs (Metabolic Equivalents).

What are METs?

  • 1 MET: The energy you burn while completely at rest (your resting metabolic rate).
  • Example: Brisk walking is typically around 4 METs, meaning you are expending four times the energy compared to when you are resting.

How it helps you:

  1. Understand your current activity levels.
  2. Nudge you with personalized and recommended activities to help you improve your score and encourage you to keep moving throughout the day.

The Sleep Index

The Sleep Index allows you to decode your sleep quality and see a breakdown of your sleep stages throughout the night. Poor sleep quality affects your metabolic health and poor metabolic health affects your sleep quality creating a feedback loop. The Ultrahuman ring lets you derive correlations between how your sleep stages like REM/NREM affect your metabolism and vice-versa, thus becoming your key to unlock the mysteries of a good night sleep and a well rested heart.

The Recovery Index / Dynamic Recovery

  • The Ring AIR's Dynamic Recovery is an aggregate measure of five parameters determining your body's preparedness for the next day and changes throughout the day. It is a percentage measure from 0-100, a higher (85 or above) number indicating better recovery.
  • The Dynamic Recovery has six factors which include sleep index, skin temperature, stress rhythm score, resting heart rate, HRV form and last night HRV.
  • Dynamic Recovery is dynamic and can be improved throughout the day by regulating stress, maintaining good sleep and sleep hygiene, proper exercise routine, eating balanced and timely meals, hydrating well and including cold therapy and active recovery.

What is the ring made of?

The Ultrahuman is designed to be tough on the outside and comfortable on the inside. The ring is crafted using:

  • A Titanium alloy billet for the body.
  • A super durable Tungsten Carbide coating which is 5 times harder than Tool Steel making it remarkably scratch-resistant and ready to take on everyday wear and tear.
  • Medical grade transparent material on the insides that have been tested safe to be used even for implanting inside the human body.
  • The ring is designed hypoallergenic and can be worn for multiple days without causing any harm to the skin.

The inside shape of the ring has multiple sections for each sensor protruding towards your finger, separated by a streamlined design with filleted edges to enable the sensors to take accurate readings while still keeping the Ultrahuman Ring comfortable enough to wear for weeks on end.

  • Smallest Ring (Size 5): Approximately 16.17 mm in diameter and 2.4 grams in weight.
  • Largest Ring (Size 14): Approximately 24.91 mm in diameter and 3.7 grams in weight.

Other Characteristics

Battery life: The battery ideally lasts from 4-6 days depending upon the usage. However, we are continuously working on optimizing the battery life of the Ultrahuman Ring with regular firmware updates to provide you with a longer-lasting experience. The battery life of 3.5 to 4 days is influenced by several factors, including ambient temperature, frequency of use, and individual usage patterns. When we advertise up to 6 days of battery life, we consider optimal conditions where the ring is worn continuously and the ambient temperature is ideal. We understand your expectations and are continuously working to optimise the ring's performance under various conditions.

Charge time: It takes around 180 minutes to fully charge the battery.

Syncing: We recommend syncing your ring at least once every 24 hours to ensure all your biosignals are safely transferred to the app and to prevent any potential loss of data.

Compatibility: The ring is compatible with Android devices running on Android 6 and above. On iOS, it is 15 and above.

Materials: The Ultrahuman ring is made of an incredibly strong Titanium body and coated with a scratch-resistant Tungsten Carbide layer. The ring is hypoallergenic, with the insides made of medical-grade transparent materials - tough on the outside, comfortable on the inside.

Content ecosystem: The Ultrahuman Ring plugs into Ultrahuman's premium content created with world-class coaches, psychologists and experts. This means we can suggest one of our workout programs that would be just perfect for you to improve your Movement Index, or something from our Sleep Series to better your Sleep Index. We can suggest one of our meditation programs based on your stress levels, Body Index and so on. We don't just give you metrics, we also provide you with world-class tools to improve your health holistically.

Bluetooth sync: The ring connects to your device via Bluetooth and transfers your biosignals to the Ultrahuman App when you open the app and sync the ring. We recommend syncing your ring at least once every 24 hours to ensure all biosignals are safely transferred and to prevent potential data loss. On iOS, the ring syncs automatically as soon as you open the Ultrahuman App. On Android, open the Ultrahuman App and manually initiate a sync to transfer the data.

Charging: The ring charges with the custom charger included in the box. It takes approx 180 minutes to charge. You can expect around 3.5-4 days of battery life on a fully charged ring.

Pricing: You can visit the Ultrahuman Ring page to see up-to-date pricing.

Support: You will also get access to a team of certified performance coaches who can help you interpret your data better and give recommendations.

The Ultra-Verse (Ultrahuman Ecosystem)

The Ultrahuman Ring seamlessly integrates with existing products like the Ultrahuman M1, creating a powerful, cohesive view of your metabolic health. By combining the Ring's sleep, movement, and body data with the M1's glucose data, you unlock deep correlations and predictive, actionable insights.

Here is how this convergence works:

  1. Movement & Glucose Data: Predictive Spikes

    The power: You receive predictive insights based on your historical glucose patterns.

    Example: If eating pizza previously caused a high glucose spike, the next time you log it, the system will recommend personalized movement goals (for example, a brisk walk) to help mitigate or "cut" the expected spike before it happens.

  2. Sleep & Glucose Data: Real-Time Recovery Impact

    The power: See in real-time how your sleep quality affects your physiological recovery (HRV) and how both variables influence glucose regulation.

    Example: If your historical data shows that poor sleep leads to higher-than-usual glucose spikes for the same meal, the Ring and M1 work together:

    • The Ring signals poor sleep/recovery.
    • The M1 confirms the higher glucose spike.
    • Cohesive Insight: You get a complete picture showing that lack of recovery is making you less insulin sensitive.
  3. HRV & Glucose Data: Stress and Metabolism

    The power: Get real-time updates on your stress levels and recovery based on your Heart Rate Variability (HRV). Paired with glucose data, you can understand the deeper correlations between emotional/physiological stress and stress-induced glucose spikes.

Individual Markers

Resting Heart Rate (RHR)

Your Resting Heart Rate (RHR) is the number of times your heart beats per minute while at rest. It’s a key indicator of your heart health, fitness, and recovery.

Why It’s Important:

  • A lower RHR usually means a stronger, more efficient heart that pumps blood with fewer beats.
  • Tracking RHR helps you understand your fitness progress and overall well-being.
  • It can be influenced by age, activity level, and genetics.

How It’s Measured:

Your RHR is best tracked while you sleep, when your body is fully at rest. This gives the most accurate picture of your heart’s baseline function. Monitoring it regularly can help you spot trends, improve recovery, and optimize health.

Heart Rate Variability (HRV)

Your Heart Rate Variability (HRV) measures the small variations in time between each heartbeat. It’s a key indicator of your body’s ability to handle stress, recover, and maintain balance.

Why It Matters:

  • A higher HRV usually means a more adaptable and resilient nervous system, helping you recover better.
  • HRV reflects the balance between your fight or flight (sympathetic) and rest and digest (parasympathetic) nervous systems.
  • Tracking HRV can help you understand stress levels, optimize recovery, and improve overall well-being.

How It Changes:

After intense exercise, HRV may temporarily drop due to physical stress, but this is part of your body’s natural adaptation. Over time, consistent exercise can improve your HRV baseline, making your body more resilient to stress and better at recovery.

Monitoring HRV regularly can help you fine-tune your workouts, stress management, and lifestyle choices for better long-term health.

Skin Temperature and Deviation

Your Skin Temperature is an important indicator of how your body responds to changes in your environment, metabolism, and overall health.

Why It Matters:

  • Skin temperature can fluctuate due to ambient temperature, circadian rhythms, metabolism, or even stress and illness.
  • Sudden changes may signal your body fighting infection, inflammation, or other physiological shifts.
  • Tracking these variations can help you stay proactive about your well-being.

How It’s Measured:

A finger-based sensor detects skin temperature trends, but it differs from core body temperature (like a thermometer reading) since it’s influenced by blood flow and external conditions.

Temperature Deviation & Your Baseline:

The Temperature Deviation factor shows how much your skin temperature deviates from your baseline. This is a key indicator of your body’s natural balance and is displayed directly on your home screen for easy tracking.

By consistently monitoring your skin temperature and its deviation, you can spot patterns, detect potential health concerns early, and optimize your recovery.

VO2 Max

VO2 Max, or maximal oxygen consumption, represents the highest rate at which your body can use oxygen during exercise. It is measured in milliliters of oxygen per minute per kilogram of body weight (mL/kg/min).

Why It Matters:

  • Best indicator of cardiovascular fitness & endurance.
  • Reflects how efficiently your body delivers and utilizes oxygen.
  • Higher VO2 Max = Better stamina & athletic performance.

Tracking VO2 Max helps you understand your fitness level, optimize training, and improve overall endurance.

Sleep Index

The Ultrahuman Ring AIR boasts a sleek, ultra-light design that pairs seamlessly with advanced sleep-tracking algorithms, ensuring you rest with ease.

Its Sleep Index provides deep insights into different sleep stages—Deep Sleep, REM, Light Sleep, and Wakefulness—each playing a vital role in your body’s growth, repair, and restoration.

By understanding the science of sleep, you gain the power to make informed decisions for better rest and overall well-being. Embrace the insights from the Sleep Index to nurture a mindful sleep routine, unlocking renewed energy, recovery, and balance every night.

Workout Mode

The Ultrahuman Ring AIR features a dedicated Workout Mode, enabling precise heart rate tracking during active workouts. This real-time monitoring helps you:

Track Heart Rate & Zones – Gain insights into your cardiovascular performance as your workout is segmented into five heart rate zones:

  • 🔵 Zone 1 – Light activity (<132 BPM)
  • 🟢 Zone 2 – Moderate intensity (132-142 BPM)
  • 🟡 Zone 3 – Endurance-building (142-159 BPM)
  • 🟠 Zone 4 – High-intensity effort (158-179 BPM)
  • 🔴 Zone 5 – Maximum exertion (>179 BPM)

Tracking these zones helps you fine-tune your training, optimize endurance, and push your limits effectively.

  • Monitor Calories Burned – Get real-time insights into your energy expenditure, helping you optimize workouts for better fitness outcomes.
  • Heart Rate Recovery – Assess how quickly your heart rate returns to baseline post-workout, a key indicator of cardiovascular fitness & recovery efficiency.
  • Note: Workouts can be automatically detected by your ring. However, to ensure your workout is recorded, you can always choose to log it manually as well for added accuracy.

If your firmware version is 8.72 or higher, Workout Mode is fully enabled on your ring. For earlier firmware versions, the feature may still appear in beta and could show occasional fluctuations.

Circadian Rhythm

Your circadian rhythm is your body’s internal clock, regulating your sleep-wake cycle, energy levels, and overall health. The Phase Response Curve (PRC) helps you understand how external factors like light, food, and exercise influence this rhythm, allowing you to optimize your sleep schedule naturally.

How It Works:

Your body follows four phases throughout the day—Circadian Dead Zone, Phase Advance, Phase Delay, and Minima. The key reference point in this cycle is your temperature minimum (the lowest core body temperature during sleep), which determines whether external stimuli will shift your wake-up time earlier or later.

Phase Advance Zone:

If you expose yourself to sunlight, eat, or exercise during this window, your body adapts by waking up earlier over time—making early mornings feel effortless.

Phase Delay Zone:

To maintain a stable sleep cycle, avoid habits that can push your bedtime later during this window:

  • ✔️ Have an early dinner
  • ✔️ Avoid late-night workouts
  • ✔️ Reduce screen time & bright light exposure
  • ✔️ Create a relaxing bedtime routine
  • ✔️ Keep your room dark for better sleep

Why It Matters

By consistently aligning with your circadian rhythm, you can enhance sleep quality, boost energy levels, and improve overall well-being. Instead of forcing a sleep schedule, allow your body to naturally adapt by waking up at the right time and letting sleep follow effortlessly. This cycle is unique to each person, and with time, your body will find its optimal rhythm.

In-App Ring and Add-Ons Transfer Flow

The In-App Ring Transfer Flow allows users to transfer their Ultrahuman Ring purchase (and all associated add-ons) to their in-app account — in case they’ve logged in with a different email ID than the one used for purchase.

Context

Our app supports limited login options based on platform:

  • iOS: Gmail and Apple ID
  • Android: Gmail and Facebook

If a user purchased the Ultrahuman Ring using an email domain other than these supported login options (for example, a work email or a different provider), they can now easily transfer their purchase to their active in-app account.

How It Works
  1. Open the Ultrahuman App.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Scroll down and select Transfer Ring and Add-Ons.
  4. Enter the purchase email ID (the one used at checkout).
  5. Verify the ownership via the email verification link sent to that ID.

Once verified, the app automatically transfers:

  • The Ultrahuman Ring order
  • Associated add-ons (like Power Plugs, Ultrahuman X, and any subscriptions)
Supported Add-Ons and Orders

We’ve expanded this feature’s capability beyond the Ring itself. Now, users can also transfer all linked purchases, including:

  • Ultrahuman X
  • Cardio subscription
  • AFib subscription
  • Any other add-ons bought along with the Ring

Users can choose to transfer either:

  • Only their last order, or
  • All previous orders

This selection is made through the verification email flow.

What This Means

If a user completes the Ring transfer process, all corresponding add-on subscriptions associated with that order will be automatically moved as well — ensuring a seamless ownership and experience continuity across devices and platforms.

Ring Discomfort

Applicable Regions: Global

Related Tags: RING_DISCOMFORT

Overview

This document outlines how to handle user complaints related to ring discomfort, rashes, or any form of physical harm with empathy, urgency, and consistency. These cases are high sensitivity and require careful communication and proper escalation.

1. General Context: Ring Discomfort & Skin Reactions

  • In very rare cases, users may experience major rashes or severe skin reactions while wearing the ring.
  • Most discomfort-related cases are not severe, but every report must still be treated with seriousness and empathy.
  • Possible contributing factors can include:
    • Moisture or humidity getting trapped between the ring and the finger
    • Prolonged wear without drying the skin
    • Individual skin sensitivity or allergies
Key tone guideline

Always be empathetic, calm, and reassuring. Avoid dismissive language, even if the issue appears minor.

2. Resolution Approach (Case-by-Case)

Each case should be evaluated individually. Possible resolutions may include:

  • Offering generic care advice (for mild irritation):
    • Keeping the finger dry
    • Removing the ring periodically
    • Avoiding wear when moisture is present
  • Recommending medical consultation when symptoms appear concerning
  • Processing a refund, depending on severity and context
  • In some cases, we may offer the user a full refund while allowing them to keep the ring

Never promise a resolution without internal alignment. Always assess severity first.

3. High-Severity Cases: Physical Harm or Injury

These situations are considered critical and require immediate escalation:

  • Tight or stuck rings (this is a medical emergency and can lead to loss of circulation or finger damage)
  • Burns or severe rashes
  • Electrical shock complaints
  • Any report of:
    • Pain
    • Swelling
    • Numbness
    • Visible injury

4. Mandatory Actions for High-Severity Cases

When a case involving physical harm or injury is reported:

  1. Do NOT respond immediately on your own - Loop in your SME or Team Lead right away.
  2. Review carefully - Go through all prior user interactions and check internal notes thoroughly.
  3. Follow internal guidelines - Refer to the official internal documentation on handling physical harm cases.
  4. Craft the response with extreme care - Lead with empathy and concern, reassure the user, maintain a calm, professional, and accountable tone, and avoid defensive or dismissive language.

Put yourself in the user’s position. The goal is to de-escalate, reassure, and ensure safety.

5. Why This Matters

  • These cases directly impact user safety and brand trust.
  • Poor handling can escalate fear or frustration.
  • Thoughtful, empathetic communication can significantly calm the situation and reinforce trust.

6. Final Reminder

Please stay vigilant, empathetic, and aligned when handling these cases. When in doubt:

  • Pause
  • Escalate
  • Ask for guidance

Handling these situations correctly protects both the user and the company.

Trade-In Process (Step-by-Step)

Purchase

  • The user places an order for the Ultrahuman Ring AIR at the full retail price.
  • During checkout, the user selects the trade-in option.
  • Trade-in product details (variant, size, model, etc.) are recorded on our end.

Delivery of New Device

  • We ship the Ultrahuman Ring AIR to the user as a standard order.
  • No trade-in ring needs to be shipped by the user at this stage.

Initiating the Return of the Old Device

  • Once the user receives the Ultrahuman Ring AIR, they contact Ultrahuman Support.
  • The support team assists in generating an RTO (Return to Origin) request.
  • The user will send their trade-in device to our nearest warehouse.

Verification

  • After we receive the device, the team inspects it.
  • If the device satisfies the trade-in acceptance criteria, it is approved.

Refund of Trade-In Credit

  • Once approved, the trade-in credit refund is initiated to the user's original payment method.
  • The refund may take 7-10 business days to reflect in their bank account.

How to Request an RTO in OMS

  1. Go to Ring Orders in the Ops dashboard.
  2. Select the order for which you want to initiate the RTO.
  3. Click Initiate RTO.
  4. Choose Trade-In from the reason dropdown.
  5. Fill in the necessary fields (device type, variant, user remarks, etc.).
  6. Submit the request.

Overview

This process is used to create and track Ultrahuman Ring replacements on OMS.

Follow this flow only after confirming the user is eligible under the Ring Replacement Policy.

Where to process replacements

Go to OMS: Order Management → Ring Orders → Request Replacement

https://ops.ultrahuman.com/admin/ring_orders/collect_replacement_lookup

Standard replacement flow

  1. Click Request Replacement.
  2. Enter the Serial Number or UHR.
  3. Select the Replacement Reason.
  4. Confirm the correct Packaging Option is selected.
    • Old packaging with charger
    • New packaging without charger

    OMS usually auto-selects the packaging based on the replacement reason, but always double-check manually.

    If the charger is not required, OMS will auto-select the new replacement box.

    The following replacement reasons will still trigger OMS to select the old box (ring + charger):

    • incorrect_sku_requested_by_cx
    • pre_selected_sizing_mismatch
    • ring_loose_vs_sizing_kit
    • ring_tight_vs_sizing_kit
    • ring_lost
    • wrong_sku_sent
    • weight_loss_coverage

    For all other cases, the new box will be shipped by default.

    Exception: If the user mentions their charger is not working or they need a replacement charger, manually select the old box so both the ring and charger are shipped.

    Also, manual intervention is required when the reason selected is battery/connection, but the user is actually requesting a size change, since OMS may choose the wrong box.

  5. Review all details to ensure they are correct.
  6. Do not click Initiate RTO inside this flow except for India orders.

RTO rule

  • Do not use the inner Initiate RTO checkbox from the replacement page (see screenshot) except for India.

For all other regions where RTO is required, use the outer Initiate RTO flow so the correct courier partner and return type are selected:

MCF orders handling

What are MCF regions?

MCF regions are locations where Amazon Multi-Channel Fulfillment ships Ultrahuman orders directly from local Amazon warehouses.

  • Orders are serviced via the EU/UK Shopify store
  • Fulfilled through Amazon MCF
  • Applies to both rings and sensors
Benefits for users
  • Lower VAT and reduced shipping costs
  • Faster delivery due to local fulfillment
  • No international customs delays

You can find the list of countries here (MCF vs Non MCF bifurcation).

Important notes about MCF orders

For MCF orders, the serial number cannot be used to locate user details.

MCF serial numbers often map to a dummy/manual order in OMS.

Always locate and process replacements using the actual user order, not the manual order linked to the serial number.

  1. If the user confirms the ring was purchased from our website and the serial search shows a manual order:
    • Example email format: shantnu+amazon-eufba@ultrahuman.com
    • Search using the user’s purchase email ID to locate the actual order.
  2. If needed, ask the user for the invoice:
    • The invoice will be from Ultrahuman
    • Use the Shopify order ID to search on OMS

All replacements must be processed on the actual Shopify order, not the manual order linked to the serial number.

This ensures:

  • Replacement history is tracked correctly
  • Warranty validation remains accurate
  • No duplicate replacements occur

Size Protection

If your ring size changes within 1 year of purchase, we offer an exchange for your RARE Ring to the nearest size, provided you meet the eligibility criteria.

Eligibility Criteria

  • It is only applicable for original owner who has purchased from an authorised retailer / outlet. Proof of purchase for the RARE Ring is required to initiate the size protection claim.
  • If the ring changes hands and/or is resold to someone else, the size protection will not be applicable if it has already been availed on that ring before once, despite the customer having the invoice.
  • Ultrahuman team will send a sizing kit to the customer to confirm the updated Ring size before proceeding with the replacement.
  • Once the new size is confirmed, Ultrahuman will ship an AIR Ring (in the revised size) for temporary use, ensuring the customer can continue tracking data while their Rare Ring is being under process.
  • The policy does not cover damages or technical issues unrelated to size changes.

Process Overview

Customer Actions:

  1. Initiate Request: Contact Ultrahuman support through the designated portal or helpline.
  2. Provide Details:
    • Share the original invoice and Ring ID.
    • Address details to ship the sizing kit.
    • Confirm new size via the sizing kit.
  3. Prepare for Shipping (only after revised size is approved): Pack the Ring securely following instructions provided by Ultrahuman.

Ultrahuman Actions:

  1. Verify details: check that the invoice is original, and Ring ID and see if it is marked to Rare Ring and given customer details.
  2. Ship sizing kit to the customer: Customer must try the latest size and confirm.
  3. Verification of size change: Confirm the revised size submitted by the customer.
  4. Arrange return shipping: Provide a prepaid shipping label and explain shipping instructions.
    1. To be shipped to our warehouse in that region (US, UK, EU and if not then UAE Office) and the authenticity to be checked by checking the Hallmark ID and if it matches our system then purity tested.
  5. Inspection:
    1. Authenticate the metal of the returned Ring - our warehouse team to check the Hallmark ID.
    2. Test for damages beyond normal wear and tear - UH team to do that.
    3. Bring it back to India.
  6. Approve replacement: Ensure the request aligns with the eligibility criteria.
  7. Replacement shipping: Dispatch a replacement Ring in the customer's updated size along with a thank you card for their patience.

Timeline

  • Shipping of sizing kit: 5-7 days.
  • Testing of the revised size by customer: 3-4 days.
  • Verification of size change evidence: 3-5 days.
  • Return shipping to Ultrahuman: 5-7 days.
  • Ring inspection and processing: 3-5 days.
  • Replacement shipping to customer: 5-7 days.
  • Total Timeline: ~25-30 days.
